Who I am

A quick introduction - the fuller version lives on my LinkedIn.

I have currently progressed to the third year of my Mechanical Engineering degree at the University of Nottingham, Malaysia, building the technical foundation that sits behind every machine I want to design.

SolidWorks is where most of my free design time goes. Recent builds include a full multi-body offshore speedboat with a hydrodynamic hull, cockpit layout and production-grade material, and a vintage biplane modelled from scratch - projects I use to practice the surfacing and assembly skills a textbook alone won't teach.

I do my best work in a team, and I've had my time as the Logistics Manager for the Sri Lankan Cultural Society at UNM, and previously sat on my hall committee coordinating events end to end. Outside of engineering, I'm a huge cricket fan - and lately I've been getting properly pulled into Formula 1 too.
